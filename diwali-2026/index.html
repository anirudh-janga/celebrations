The almanac

Diwali 2026 in India

Diwali fell on Sunday, 8 November, on the new-moon night of Kartik Amavasya. There's no moon on Diwali, so every lamp counts.

The five days

  1. 6Fri
    DhanterasNew gold, silver and steel utensils come home for luck.
  2. 7Sat
    Naraka ChaturdashiThe big day in Telugu homes: an early oil bath and crackers before dawn, for Satyabhama's victory over Narakasura. Ayodhya's Deepotsav that evening.
  3. 8Sun
    Deepavali Amavasya · Lakshmi PujaEvening puja, diyas at every door. Kali Puja in Bengal, Bandi Chhor Divas for Sikhs.
  4. 10Tue
    Govardhan Puja · Annakut · Bali PadyamiA mountain of food offered to Krishna.
  5. 11Wed
    Bhagini Hasta Bhojanam · Bhai DoojBrothers eat a meal cooked by their sisters, and bring the gifts.

Amavasya ran into the morning of 9 November, so most panchangs moved Govardhan Puja and Bhai Dooj by a day. Some calendars list them on 9 and 10 November. Check your family's panchang.

What happened this year

UNESCO The first Diwali as world heritage

On 10 December 2025, at UNESCO's committee session held at the Red Fort in Delhi, Deepavali was added to the list of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity. It is India's 16th entry, alongside Kumbh Mela, Durga Puja and Garba.

Source: All India Radio
Ayodhya · 7 November
30 lakh+ diyas
A river of lamps for the 10th Deepotsav

The Saryu ghats glowed with more than 30 lakh earthen lamps. The UP Chief Minister put the count at 36 lakh, well past the 2025 Guinness record of 26,17,215. A Ramayana drone show drew Ram Darbar and the Pushpak Viman across the sky.

Sources: Business Standard, News24
Delhi-NCR Green crackers, two-hour windows

The Supreme Court allowed only certified green crackers on 7 and 8 November, from 6 to 7 am and 8 to 10 pm. Ladis, garlands and barium crackers stayed banned, and the full ban returned on 9 November.

Sources: The Hans India, CourtBook
Markets
₹1.25 lakh crore
A record Dhanteras

Traders' body CAIT estimated a record day of Dhanteras business, with gold and silver alone above ₹66,000 crore. It expects Diwali sales to cross ₹7 lakh crore, with Delhi above ₹1 lakh crore. These are early estimates.

Sources: IANS, The Hans India
Same night, 8 November Kali Puja and Bandi Chhor Divas

Bengal worshipped Ma Kali through the night at Kalighat and Dakshineswar, while Sikh families marked Bandi Chhor Divas, the day Guru Hargobind Ji was freed from Gwalior Fort.

Source: BankBazaar holiday calendar
On the border Diwali with the troops

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh spent Diwali with soldiers in Sikkim, and President Droupadi Murmu sent her greetings to the nation.

Source: ETV Bharat live updates

Light them safely

  • Buy green crackers (look for the CSIR-NEERI logo) and follow local time limits.
  • Keep a bucket of water and sand nearby. Drop used sparklers in the water.
  • Wear cotton, light at arm's length, and never go back to a dud.
  • Keep pets indoors, and go easy near hospitals and the elderly.
