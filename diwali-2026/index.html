The almanac

Diwali 2026 in India

Diwali fell on Sunday, 8 November, the night of the new moon. With no moon in the sky, every lamp counts.

The five days

  1. 6Fri
    DhanterasShopping day for gold, silver and new pots.
  2. 7Sat
    Naraka Chaturdashi · Choti DiwaliThe big day in Telugu homes: an early oil bath and fireworks before sunrise. Ayodhya's lamp festival.
  3. 8Sun
    DiwaliPrayers to Lakshmi in the evening and lamps at every door.
  4. 10Tue
    Govardhan PujaA mountain of food offered to Krishna.
  5. 11Wed
    Bhai Dooj · Bhagini Hasta BhojanamBrothers eat a meal cooked by their sisters and bring gifts.

Some calendars put Govardhan Puja and Bhai Dooj a day earlier, on 9 and 10 November.

What happened this year

UNESCO The first Diwali as world heritage

In December 2025, UNESCO added Diwali to its list of the world's cultural heritage. It is India's 16th entry, alongside Kumbh Mela, Durga Puja and Garba.

Source: All India Radio
Ayodhya · 7 November
3 million+ lamps
A river of lamps

Ayodhya's riverbanks glowed with more than 3 million clay lamps. Officials put the count near 3.6 million, beating last year's world record of about 2.6 million. A drone show told the Ramayana story in the sky.

Sources: Business Standard, News24
Delhi-NCR Green fireworks, two hours a night

The Supreme Court allowed only "green" fireworks on 7 and 8 November, from 6 to 7 am and 8 to 10 pm. Cracker chains stayed banned, and the full ban came back on 9 November.

Sources: The Hans India, CourtBook
Markets
₹1.25 trillion
A record shopping day

Traders' group CAIT estimated a record Dhanteras, with gold and silver alone above ₹660 billion. It expects Diwali sales to pass ₹7 trillion. These are early estimates.

Sources: IANS, The Hans India
Same night, 8 November Kali Puja and Bandi Chhor Divas

Bengal celebrated Kali Puja through the night, and Sikh families marked Bandi Chhor Divas.

Source: BankBazaar holiday calendar
On the border Diwali with the troops

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh spent Diwali with soldiers in Sikkim, and President Droupadi Murmu sent her greetings to the nation.

Source: ETV Bharat live updates

Light them safely

  • Buy green fireworks and follow local time limits.
  • Keep a bucket of water and sand nearby. Drop used sparklers in the water.
  • Wear cotton, light at arm's length, and never go back to a dud.
  • Keep pets indoors, and go easy near hospitals and the elderly.
